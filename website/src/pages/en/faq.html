FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How to move and resize the window, where your data goes — answers to the common questions about PinShell.

Is PinShell free?

Yes — free and open source under Apache 2.0, with no account, subscription, or in-app purchase. The first-party templates in the online library are free too, and update independently.

Is my usage data uploaded anywhere?

No. PinShell has no account system and no telemetry. Pages run locally from file://, and the MCP server and CLI talk over a local control.sock. Template files are downloaded from this site only when you install one; some templates (stocks, AI radio) call public data APIs on their own.

How do I make my own template?

No coding needed: copy the configuration from Console → MCP and paste it into an MCP-capable client such as Doubao, Qwen, DeepSeek Harness, or WorkBuddy, and let the AI set it up. Then just describe the panel or change you want in the conversation — the AI creates, edits, and manages templates, with the window updating live.

How do I contribute a template to the website?

Add template.json and <id>.html under website/templates/<id>/, follow the template contribution guide for the manifest, localization, and design-token rules, and run the local validation. Then open a PR against niyongsheng/PinShell titled feat(template): add <id>. Once merged, it is published automatically to the website and the App template library.

How do I move or resize the overlay?

In Desktop Overlay mode, hold ⌘ and drag anywhere in the window to move it; drag the handle in the bottom-right corner to resize. You can also snap the window to a screen corner from the PinShell menu bar icon → Window Position. In Menu Bar Popover mode the window stays anchored to the menu bar icon and cannot be moved freely.

What is the difference between Desktop Overlay and Menu Bar Popover?

Desktop Overlay keeps the window above your desktop, free to move, resize, and click through. Menu Bar Popover attaches the template to the menu bar icon: click to show, click outside to dismiss, with a fixed position. Switch between them anytime from the PinShell menu bar icon → Display Mode.

macOS says the developer cannot be verified, or the app is damaged?

The package is ad-hoc signed and not yet notarized. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/PinShell-*.pkg on the package before installing.
